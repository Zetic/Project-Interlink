Physical diagnostics

WG-3 scope

Rust generates coherent continental, transitional, and oceanic crust independently from current plate identity, then derives crust age, thickness, density, and buoyancy.

WG-2 kinematics are reinterpreted into subduction, collision, ridge, rift, transitional-divergence, and transform regimes. Oceanic crust age is inferred from spreading distance and rate; geological-history fields propagate those causes across the spherical topology.

Plate ownership, tectonic boundaries, and rigid motion remain available as inherited diagnostics. Major/intermediate/minor plate classes are derived from physical plate area rather than generation inputs.

No elevation, bathymetry, terrain, lithology, climate, hydrology, resources, Regions, Features, or gameplay cutover are generated in WG-3.
